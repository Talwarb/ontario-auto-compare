ONTARIO INSURANCE 101

Understand the coverage before the price.

Ontario drivers must carry certain auto insurance coverages. Optional protections may help with damage to your own vehicle and other needs.

Read official Ontario insurance guidance ↗
Third-party liability

Protects against covered legal liability for injury or damage caused to others.

Accident benefits

Provides eligible benefits following an automobile accident, subject to policy terms.

Collision and comprehensive

Optional physical-damage protections; availability and deductibles vary.

FREQUENT QUESTIONS

Good questions, clear answers.

Are these actual insurance quotes?

No. All displayed dollar amounts are fictional examples for demonstration. This site currently has no live insurance quote integration.

Does this website sell insurance?

No. This starter site is an educational demo and does not sell, bind, advise on, or arrange insurance policies.

What determines my actual premium?

Insurers may consider factors such as driving history, vehicle, location, annual use, chosen coverage, and eligibility, subject to applicable Ontario rules.

How can I get a real quote?

Contact an insurer or appropriately licensed broker directly. This website will only offer live comparisons after an authorized partner integration is in place.
