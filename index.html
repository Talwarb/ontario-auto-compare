Explore life insurance types

Term life insurance

Protection for a selected term such as 10, 20, 30 or 40 years, where offered.

Often considered for time-limited financial obligations.

Whole life insurance

Permanent coverage, potentially with cash values and guarantees depending on the contract.

Features and costs vary by policy.

Universal life insurance

Permanent coverage with investment-linked features and flexible funding, subject to policy rules.

Values and costs may fluctuate.

Not yet. Real insurer prices require approved quoting access and relevant licensing and insurer authorization.

Term life insurance generally does not accumulate cash value.

Eligibility depends on insurer underwriting, product terms and provincial availability.
