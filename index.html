Frequently asked questions

Are these calculators free?

Yes. You can use them without signing up.

Are results financial advice?

No. They are general educational estimates, not tax, investment, lending or financial advice.

Does the website store my income or mortgage details?

No. Calculator inputs are processed locally in your browser.

Does the tax tool calculate exact take-home pay?

No. You provide an assumed effective tax rate; the tool does not calculate CRA tax brackets, CPP or EI.
